Surah audio download — dataSync

The user opens the Quran, selects Al-Baqarah and taps Play. The app downloads the complete Surah into private storage. The recording shows actual verse progress in the foreground-service notification while the app is backgrounded, then completion and local playback after returning.

Why foreground execution is used

The download follows an explicit Play request. Continuing in the background lets the user switch apps without interrupting offline preparation. Delaying or interrupting it leaves the selected Surah unavailable offline. Progress and a Cancel action stay visible; the service stops when the transfer finishes.
