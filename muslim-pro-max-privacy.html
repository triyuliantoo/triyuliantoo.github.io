Privacy Policy for Muslim Pro Max

Last updated: October 27, 2026

Muslim Pro Max is an ad-free Islamic utility application. It has no user accounts, advertising SDKs, or behavioral analytics SDKs. The app uses location only when you request location-based prayer times or Qibla direction.

1. Developer and scope

This policy applies to the Muslim Pro Max Android application (package id.anekaruangkode.muslimpro), developed by Tri Yulianto / Aneka Ruang Kode ("we", "our", or "us").

2. Data the app processes

3. Third-party services

The app may connect to the following services solely to provide requested content or calculations:

  • AlAdhan — prayer-time calculations for coordinates outside supported city schedules.
  • MyQuran/Kemenag schedule service — Indonesian city lists and prayer schedules.
  • EQuran.id — Quran text and surah content.
  • AlQuran Cloud — Quran translations.
  • Muslim Pro Max content service — tajweed annotations.
  • Android system geocoding — converts a device location into a readable place name when available.

These providers may process standard network data under their own privacy terms. We do not sell personal information or share it for advertising.

4. Device permissions

  • Location: optional and requested in context for location-based prayer times and Qibla direction. Manual city selection remains available.
  • Notifications and exact alarms: optional and used for prayer reminders and adhan alerts at the selected times.
  • Foreground service and audio controls: used only while adhan or other requested audio is playing.
  • Boot completed: used to restore enabled prayer reminders after device restart or app update.
  • Internet and network state: used to retrieve Quran content and prayer schedules.

5. Retention and deletion

Local preferences, bookmarks, reading progress, schedules, and the selected location remain on your device until you change them, clear the application's storage, or uninstall the app. Muslim Pro Max has no user account and therefore has no server-side account record to delete. You can revoke location or notification permissions at any time in Android Settings.

6. Security

The app uses HTTPS for network requests and limits requested permissions to its stated functionality. No method of electronic transmission is completely secure, but we take reasonable steps to minimize data use and exposure.

7. Children

The app is intended for a general audience and is not specifically directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect children's personal information through an account, advertising, or analytics system.

8. Changes

We may update this policy when the app's functionality or legal requirements change. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when changes are made.
